<h1>Drink Theory Studio</h1>

<div class="post-body" markdown="1">
Drink Theory is a research journal on how drinks work: why flavours pair, what fermentation and infusion actually do, and how small changes in technique change what ends up in the glass.

It is written by **Prasid Shetty** from a working bar in Dharamkot, high in the Indian Himalayas, where local ingredients and experiments find their way onto the menu.

New notes are published every week.
